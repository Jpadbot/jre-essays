## Prompt reminder

You work in the City of Jade Social Welfare Department. The Integrated Mental Health Centre has asked for an internal discussion paper on a recent multi-agency case. Write a clear, professional case analysis that identifies key issues, evaluates what went well and what did not, and proposes practical next steps for the centre and partner agencies.

## Model essay (English)

**Internal discussion paper**  
**Subject:** Case review — Mr L., referral pathway and multi-agency coordination  
**Prepared for:** Centre Management Meeting  
**Classification:** Internal use only

### 1. Purpose

This note summarises the handling of Mr L., a 42-year-old resident referred to the City of Jade Integrated Mental Health Centre after two A&E attendances for panic-like episodes and one police welfare check. The aim is not to assign blame, but to record what the file shows, where coordination stalled, and what the centre can change without waiting for new legislation.

### 2. Brief case outline

Mr L. lives alone in a rented room. He works irregular night shifts at a logistics depot. In August he attended A&E twice within ten days, describing chest tightness, fear of “losing control”, and insomnia. Cardiac investigations were normal. On the second visit, A&E staff suggested community follow-up and gave a leaflet for our centre. He did not call.

Three weeks later, neighbours reported shouting at night. Police attended, found no offence, and submitted a welfare referral. Our intake team contacted Mr L. by phone the next working day. He agreed to a first appointment but cancelled twice, citing shift conflicts. A community nurse eventually met him at a café near his workplace. He disclosed increasing alcohol use “to sleep”, financial pressure from rent arrears, and a fear that seeking sick leave would cost him his job. He declined a hospital psychiatric clinic slot because he believed it would appear on employment checks. He accepted brief counselling and a nurse-led sleep and breathing plan.

### 3. What worked

Three elements deserve recognition. First, the police welfare channel reached us quickly; the one-working-day callback met our service standard. Second, offering an off-site first contact reduced his anxiety about being “seen as a patient” in a clinical setting. Third, the nurse correctly separated urgent risk (which was low at that meeting) from functional decline (which was clear), and avoided overloading him with forms on day one.

### 4. Where the pathway failed

The weak points are structural, not personal. A&E discharge advice relied on the service user initiating contact. People in acute distress often cannot complete that step. There was no warm handoff—no named worker, no booked slot, no consent for A&E to share contact details with us under an agreed protocol. As a result, three weeks of untreated escalation passed before the police route brought him in.

Shift work also exposed a rigidity in our booking system. Morning-only slots and a 48-hour cancellation rule effectively punished the very clients who need flexibility. Finally, stigma around psychiatric records shaped his refusal of clinic referral. Our staff explained confidentiality, but we lacked a short, plain-language leaflet on what employers can and cannot access, and we had no same-week evening counselling alternative to offer as a bridge.

### 5. Options for the centre

**Option A — Strengthen A&E warm handoff.** Negotiate a pilot with two A&E units: with consent, a same-day or next-day centre slot is booked before discharge, and our intake SMS confirms the appointment. Cost is mainly coordination time; benefit is earlier engagement.

**Option B — Flexible access track.** Create a small evening and Saturday quota for shift workers, with telephone triage the same day as a missed appointment. This requires roster changes but targets a known local workforce pattern.

**Option C — Stigma and information pack.** Produce a one-page brief (Chinese and English) on confidentiality and employment, plus a peer-support contact list. Low cost; supports Option A and B.

A combined A+B+C pilot for six months is preferable to choosing only one. Volume at our centre is manageable if the pilot is capped at ten warm-handoff cases per month in the first quarter.

### 6. Risks and safeguards

Any faster pathway must keep risk assessment standards: suicidal ideation, harm to others, and severe self-neglect still require immediate escalation. Consent for data sharing must be explicit and recorded. We should monitor whether evening slots are used by the intended group or simply absorbed by general demand.

### 7. Recommendation

Management is invited to approve a six-month pilot combining warm handoff with A&E, a limited flexible-access track, and a bilingual confidentiality leaflet. A short review at month three should report engagement rates, DNA (did-not-attend) rates, and staff workload. The case of Mr L. shows that goodwill at each agency is not enough; the joints between agencies are where clients fall.

## Chinese translation（中文翻譯）

**內部討論文件**  
**主旨：**個案檢討——L 先生之轉介路徑與跨機構協調  
**供：**中心管理會議  
**密級：**只限內部使用

### 1. 目的

本文件概述 L 先生個案之處理過程。L 先生為四十二歲居民，於兩次急症室就診（症狀類似驚恐發作）及一次警方福利查問後，轉介至翡翠市精神健康綜合中心。本文旨在記錄檔案所見、協調停滯之處，以及中心在無需等待新法例下可立即改善的做法，而非追究個人責任。

### 2. 個案概要

L 先生獨居於分間房，於物流倉庫任不規律夜更。八月內十日兩度求診急症室，主訴胸口緊、害怕「失控」及失眠；心臟檢查正常。第二次離院時，職員建議社區跟進並派發本中心單張，惟他並無致電。

約三星期後，鄰居報稱夜間喧嘩。警方到場未發現罪行，惟提交福利轉介。本中心接收組於下一工作天致電，他同意首次面見，其後因更份衝突兩度取消。社區護士最終於其工作地附近茶餐廳會見。他透露以酒精助眠、租金欠款造成經濟壓力，並擔心放病假會失工作。他拒絕醫院精神科門診，因相信會出現於僱主查核；惟接受短期輔導及護士主導之睡眠與呼吸練習計劃。

### 3. 做得妥當之處

有三點值得肯定。第一，警方福利渠道迅速到達本中心，下一工作天回电符合服務標準。第二，安排院外首次接觸，減低他擔心「被視為病人」的焦慮。第三，護士正確區分當時較低的緊急風險與明顯的功能下滑，避免首日表格過載。

### 4. 路徑失效之處

薄弱環節屬結構性。急症室離院建議依賴服務使用者主動聯絡；急性困擾者往往無法完成此步。當中欠缺「溫暖交接」——沒有指定跟進人員、沒有已預約時段、也沒有在議定協議下經同意讓急症室向本中心分享聯絡資料。結果在警方途徑介入前，病情已惡化約三星期。

更份工作亦暴露預約制度僵化：偏向上午時段及四十八小時取消規則，變相懲罰最需要彈性的服務使用者。此外，對精神科紀錄的污名影響他拒絕門診轉介。職員雖已解釋保密，惟欠缺淺白單張說明僱主可查與不可查的範圍，亦無同周晚上輔導作為過渡選項。

### 5. 中心可採方案

**方案 A——強化急症室溫暖交接。**與兩間急症室試行：經同意後，離院前預約當日或翌日中心時段，并由接收組短訊確認。成本主要為協調時間；好處是更早接上服務。

**方案 B——彈性使用軌道。**為輪班人士預留少量晚上及周六名額，缺席當日即電話分流。需調整更表，但貼合本地勞動模式。

**方案 C——污名與資訊包。**製作中英對照一頁保密與就業說明，並附同儕支援聯絡。成本低，可支援 A、B。

宜以六個月 A+B+C 合併試點，優於只選其一。若首季每月上限十宗溫暖交接，以本中心量大致可控。

### 6. 風險與保障

加速路徑仍須維持風險評估標準：自殺意念、傷害他人及嚴重自我照顧缺失仍須即時升級處理。資料共用須明確同意並存檔。應監察晚上名額是否供目標群組使用，抑或被一般需求吸納。

### 7. 建議

請管理層批准六個月試點：急症室溫暖交接、有限彈性軌道，以及雙語保密單張。第三個月作短檢討，匯報接上率、缺席率及人手負荷。L 先生個案顯示，各機構善意並不足夠；機構接縫處正是服務使用者跌落之處。
